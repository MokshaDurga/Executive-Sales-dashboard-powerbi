# Executive Sales Dashboard | Power BI
## Project Overview
The Executive Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance across countries, product categories,  months, and sales channels. It provides key business insights through visualizations and performance indicators.

## Objectives
- Analyze total revenue and gross profit.
- Monitor gross margin, quantity sold, and total orders.
- Compare monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Identify the top and bottom countries by revenue.
- Evaluate sales channel and product category performance.

## Tools Used
- Power BI — dashboard development and data visualization
- Microsoft Excel — data source, as applicable

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Total Gross Profit, Gross Margin, Total Quantity, and Total Orders.
- **Monthly Analysis:** Revenue and gross profit trends.
- **Country Analysis:** Top and bottom five countries by revenue.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Revenue comparison across sales channels.
- **Product Analysis:** Revenue distribution across product categories.
- **Interactive Filters:** Quarter, month, country, and product category.

## How to View the Project
